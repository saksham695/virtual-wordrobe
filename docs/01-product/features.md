# Every feature, by phase

The full list from this whole idea: wardrobe, styling, packing, buying help, social, accessories, tailoring and the brand. Ordered so each phase only ships what the previous one earned.

__Core habit __Earns money __Social / growth __Supporting

## Phase 0 — the core habit21 features

Month 1. Nothing here needs payments, social or inventory. Ship only this.

### Getting clothes in

  1. **Bulk photo upload** Multi-select 10+ at once from camera or gallery; items appear as they finish processing.
  2. **Auto background cutout** On-device (iOS subject lift, Android ML Kit), server fallback. Free and instant.
  3. **AI auto-tagging** Category, colour, pattern, fabric, formality, season, layer role — one vision call per item.
  4. **One-tap tag correction** Low-confidence tags highlighted for a quick fix; corrections improve the prompt over time.
  5. **Gallery scan for existing photos** Finds photos where you're wearing clothes and extracts items, so the closet starts half-full.
  6. **Manual add and edit** For items that photograph badly or need custom notes.

### The closet

  7. **Category grid** Separate men's and women's category sets; sets and layers handled natively.
  8. **Search and filters** Colour, category, formality, season, unworn.
  9. **Laundry status** Mark in-wash; excluded from every suggestion until returned. The "it knows my life" feature.
  10. **Archive and fit reasons** Retire items with a reason (doesn't fit, worn out) — becomes tailoring demand data later.
  11. **Item detail with wear history** Times worn, last worn, what it pairs with.

### Outfits and styling

  12. **What goes with this?** Tap any item, see ranked matches for every slot. Pure database lookup, zero AI cost.
  13. **Wardrobe feed** Endless scroll of outfit cards built only from your clothes. Replaces Instagram scrolling.
  14. **Feed filters** Occasion, colour, weather, gym, ethnic, "not worn in 30 days".
  15. **Save / skip learning** Double-tap saves, swipe skips; both teach the engine your taste.
  16. **Occasion stylist (AI)** Pick office, party, date, wedding function, festival, gym; get 3 looks with one-line reasons.
  17. **Free-text occasion note** "Rooftop birthday, black jeans in wash" — maps to the nearest occasion profile.
  18. **Weather-aware picks** Uses your city's forecast for today's suggestions.
  19. **Forgotten-item nudges** "Unworn for 120 days — here are 3 ways to wear it." This is the emotional hook of the whole app.
  20. **Wore-this-today tracking** Feeds wear counts, repeat-avoidance and laundry.
  21. **Shareable outfit card** Clean flat-lay image with your logo, sized for Stories. Free marketing per share.

## Phase 1 — usefulness and first revenue12 features

Months 2–3, only if Phase 0 retention holds. Affiliate before subscription.

  22. **Trip and vacation packing** Destination + dates + trip type → capsule packing list and day-by-day outfits, weather-matched, shoe-count minimised.
  23. **Packing gap alerts** "No light jacket for Manali evenings" — useful, and a natural buying moment.
  24. **Should I buy this?** Paste a Myntra/Ajio/Amazon link or screenshot: how many new outfits it unlocks, what it pairs with, duplicate warnings.
  25. **Gap finder** "White sneakers would add 12 outfits" — ranked by outfits unlocked, with affiliate links.
  26. **Subscription (₹100/month)** 30 AI looks a month; yearly plan pushed hardest. Free tier keeps feed and pairings.
  27. **Occasion packs (₹29–49)** One-off purchases for non-subscribers; spikes in wedding and festival season.
  28. **Skin-tone and body profile** Colour and fit rules personalised to the user — turns "these match" into "this suits you".
  29. **Morning notification** Weather + 3 outfits + tap to mark worn, in under 15 seconds. The habit anchor.
  30. **Voice / chat styling** "Party tonight, nothing ironed" → outfits. Runs on the same rules engine.
  31. **Monthly wrapped** "You rediscovered 7 items and wore 12 new combinations." Highly shareable.
  32. **Closet stats** Most and least worn, cost-per-wear if price is entered, colour balance.
  33. **Resale hook** Items unworn 90+ days → list on a partner resale platform. Users earn; you take a referral cut.

## Phase 2 — social and creators9 features

Months 4–6, only once there are enough good wardrobes worth following.

  34. **Public outfits (opt-in)** Per-outfit publishing, never the whole closet. Private stays the default.
  35. **Follow wardrobes** Browse other people's looks; save ideas to your own.
  36. **Recreate with my clothes** See someone's outfit rebuilt from your wardrobe, showing only the missing piece. The killer social feature.
  37. **Creator affiliate split** Follower buys a tagged item → creator gets a share of your commission.
  38. **Creator dashboard** Views, saves, clicks, earnings; KYC and payouts via a payments provider.
  39. **Virtual try-on** See a forgotten shirt on your own body in a new combination. Premium, image-generation cost per render.
  40. **Style challenges** "30 outfits from 15 clothes" — community events that produce content for you.
  41. **Moderation and safety** Report, block, and review queue. Non-negotiable before any social launch.
  42. **Affiliate disclosure labels** Automatic tagging of commission-earning outfits, per Indian influencer advertising rules.

## Phase 3 — commerce and the brand7 features

Months 7–12+, and where you likely need a fashion or retail co-founder.

  43. **Finishing-touch accessories** One suggestion per outfit where a slot is empty: belt, watch, earrings, scarf. No size risk, easy yes.
  44. **Partner D2C storefronts** Small Indian accessory brands listed in-app on commission; no inventory for you.
  45. **Own accessory line** Private label once click data shows what sells. The real first step of the clothing brand.
  46. **Tailoring referrals** "Fix the fit" on badly-fitting items → partner doorstep tailors, referral cut, zero operations.
  47. **Saved measurements** Stored once, reused for tailoring and size guidance. Reserve the field early, ask later.
  48. **Made-to-order ethnic wear** Occasion looks with a missing piece → get it stitched. Natural fit with your ethnic wear work.
  49. **Anonymised brand insights** What people in a city actually own and wear. Valuable to brands, needs scale and explicit consent.

## Parked — separate business1

Good idea, wrong product. Test it standalone in a weekend, not inside this app.

  50. **Creator PR-surplus marketplace** New, sealed PR items sold at deep discounts. Different business (trust, payments, logistics, tax), shares only the audience. Test one manual Instagram drop before building anything.

**How to read this list:** the green items are the only ones that matter in month one. If features 12, 13, 16 and 19 don't create a daily habit, nothing below them is worth building. Everything after Phase 0 is earned, not planned.
