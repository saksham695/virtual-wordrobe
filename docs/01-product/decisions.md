# Decisions log

Every decision taken after `prd.md` was written. Newest at the bottom. Each one amends the PRD;
where they disagree, the decision wins. IDs are stable — refer to them in commits and tasks.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Amends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | 2026-09-22 | **Affiliate is out of V1 and V1-B.** No commission-bearing links anywhere. The gap finder ("white sneakers would add 12 outfits") stays as pure advice. | You cannot credibly tell someone "you already own enough" while taking a cut when they buy more. The two goals fought; now they don't. Positioning becomes shop-your-own-closet, which has a real audience that is hostile to the affiliate version. | prd §11 "Better gap" + affiliate edge case; features #25, #37 |
| D02 | 2026-09-22 | **The product's core object is the hidden outfit.** The headline number — "You own N outfits. You've worn M." — is a first-class metric: computed by the engine, shown on Closet header and Profile, tracked as `hidden_outfits_seen` in analytics, and used in the share card. | People own combinations they have never noticed. That waste is arithmetic, and arithmetic makes a hook; it is the pitch, the payoff, the retention loop and the reel in one line. The original PRD never said anything that sharp. | prd §1 goals, §3 IA, §8 feed, §13 events |
| D03 | 2026-09-22 | **The feed gets a novelty objective alongside rotation.** Anchor mix becomes 30% never-worn-together combinations, 30% least-recently-worn items, 20% favourites' neighbours, 20% random. Each card carries a `novelty` flag when no item pair in it has a `wear` event together. | The PRD's feed was a rotation engine (resurfaces forgotten *items*). Discovery of surprising *combinations* is a different objective and nothing optimised for it. | prd §8 generation rules; engine.md §feed |
| D04 | 2026-09-22 | **Outfit quality gets a measured acceptance gate: the styling bench.** 50 outfits generated from one real 40-item closet, rated 1–5 by 5 people; recorded floor; no beta ship below it. Three engines are scored against it before choosing: hand rules, off-the-shelf embedding cosine, vision-LLM ranking. | Tagging had a 100-photo test and an 80% bar; feed rendering had a 1.5 s bar; outfit quality — the whole product — had nothing. Goal 2 (60% save rate) would only be discovered in week 4 with real users, far too late. | prd §1, §5 acceptance; new `06-ai/bench.md` |
| D05 | 2026-09-22 | **Goal 4 (≥30% try buy-check) is removed as a V1 success criterion.** Buy-check remains V1-B, built last, cut first. Its adoption is a Phase 1 signal, not a V1 gate. | A success criterion cannot sit on the feature the same document pre-agrees to drop. | prd §1 goal 4 |
| D06 | 2026-09-22 | **Detection accuracy is a research risk, tested before UI is built.** 20 real flat-lay photos (10 men's, 10 women's, ethnic-heavy) through the chosen detection path *before* the review screen exists. If <60% at that point, the primary add path becomes gallery-scan + single-item, and the box editor is deferred. | ≥80% multi-item detection on messy flat-lays including sarees and dupattas was stated as an acceptance criterion but is really an assumption. If it fails, the ten-minute promise dies and the only fallback in the PRD was the exact friction the design exists to avoid. | prd §5 acceptance; build-plan T02 |
| D07 | 2026-09-22 | **Every save, skip and wear writes pair-label rows** (`pair_labels`: item_a, item_b, label, source, occurred_at). Kept from day one even though V1 trains nothing. | A labelled Indian outfit-compatibility dataset does not exist publicly. Whering/Acloset don't have one. This is the one asset that compounds — the thing the Incorporate engine scored 0/2 on. It costs one table. | schema.md; F08 |
| D08 | 2026-09-22 | **V1 ranking is rules; V2 path is type-aware embeddings** (Vasileva et al. 2018 style: one embedding per item, computed at upload in the same pass as tagging; compatibility = learned distance per type-pair). Slot for `items.embedding vector(512)` is reserved in the schema, nullable, unused in V1. | Fits the precomputed-pairing architecture exactly: one vector per item at write time, microsecond distances at read time, zero per-card AI. Not trained in V1 because training is months and the bench (D04) must first show rules are insufficient. | engine.md §v2; schema.md |
| D09 | 2026-09-22 | **The `bodies-like-yours` research does not transfer.** Its docs (couture, cloth, hair, skin, rig) are single-garment fit realism, not multi-item compatibility. Only `B-couture.md` §3 (proportion/drape) informs the fit-balance rule. | Verified by grep: one hit for "compatibility", about rig skeletons. | — |
| D10 | 2026-09-24 | **Screens are designed before architecture is finalised.** Canvas: see `02-ux/screens.md` header. 12 screens, phone 390×844, linked as a tappable prototype. | Two of the three things that decide the product — whether detection review feels like ten minutes, and whether an outfit card reads as an outfit — are interaction questions no schema answers. | — |
| D11 | 2026-09-24 | **Visual identity:** Archivo (variable width) + JetBrains Mono for numerals; warm paper ground; denim / khaki / olive / tape palette; garments drawn as flat silhouettes in mockups; no fake photos, no fake status bars. | Continuity with the product docs; distinctive against the white-and-purple AI-app default; readable in Indian daylight. | `02-ux/design-system.md` |
| D12 | 2026-09-24 | **Outfit cards are composed, not gridded.** Scale hierarchy (top largest, bottom slightly overlapping, footwear and accessories small), slight rotation, a ground shadow.
